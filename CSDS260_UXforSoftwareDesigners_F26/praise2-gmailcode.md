* WHAT: Previously, retrieving a two-factor authentication (2FA) One-Time Password (OTP) required switching apps, opening the Gmail inbox, loading the specific email, visually scanning a wall of text to locate the digits, and manually highlighting or memorizing the code to paste it back into the login screen. By parsing incoming verification emails automatically and surfacing a prominent "Copy code" action button directly on the push notification and inbox preview, Gmail eliminates four unnecessary navigation steps and prevents context-switching errors or highlight-slippage. It works seamlessly because it recognizes that for an OTP email, the user's sole objective is extracting those specific digits rather than reading the message body.
